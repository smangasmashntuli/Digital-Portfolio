## Reflection (STAR Method)

**Situation:** I received a job offer from OmniHR Consulting for a position I was very interested in. This required a formal and professional response to accept the offer.

**Task:** My task was to craft a professional acceptance email that expressed genuine enthusiasm for the opportunity while maintaining appropriate business etiquette and confirming my acceptance clearly.

**Action:** I composed a concise yet warm email that immediately expressed gratitude for the offer. I used positive language to convey my excitement ("absolutely thrilled," "dreaming of the chance," "genuinely excited") while keeping the tone professional. I made sure to explicitly state my acceptance and mention looking forward to collaborating with the team.

**Result:** The email successfully communicated my acceptance in a professional manner that left a positive impression. It demonstrated my ability to conduct formal business correspondence appropriately, which is crucial for workplace communication. This experience helped me understand the importance of tone and clarity in professional emails.

# Interview Skills

## Evidence
**Interview Preparation & Practice**

I was invited to interview by two companies:
- OmniHR Consulting
- African Youth Ignited

While there were no specific questions provided beforehand for practice, I proactively researched common interview questions online. The question I focused on practicing was "Tell us more about yourself," which is a fundamental interview question that requires careful preparation to present oneself effectively.

## Reflection (STAR Method)

**Situation:** I had upcoming interviews with two different organizations but lacked specific information about the exact questions they would ask. This required comprehensive preparation to handle various potential questions effectively.

**Task:** My task was to prepare thoroughly for these interviews by researching common questions, practicing my responses, and developing strategies to present my skills and experience compellingly.

**Action:** I identified "Tell us more about yourself" as a critical opening question and developed a structured response that highlighted my educational background, technical skills, and career aspirations. I practiced this response multiple times to ensure it was natural and confident. I also researched other common behavioral and technical questions relevant to ICT roles.

**Result:** Through this preparation, I gained confidence in articulating my value proposition to potential employers. The practice helped me understand how to structure my responses effectively and present myself as a competent candidate. This skill is transferable to any future interview situation.

## Evidence

Through my work logbook, my mentor has reviewed and provided feedback on my workplace conduct, professional behavior, and overall etiquette in professional settings.

## Reflection (STAR Method)

**Task:** My task was to demonstrate appropriate workplace etiquette, receive constructive feedback professionally, and implement suggestions for improvement in my professional conduct.

When receiving feedback about my workplace demeanor and professional behavior, I listened attentively, asked clarifying questions, and developed an action plan to address areas needing improvement. I implemented the suggestions consistently in my daily work interactions.

**Result:** The mentor's feedback has significantly improved my understanding of professional workplace expectations. I've developed stronger professional relationships and demonstrated growth in my workplace conduct. This ongoing process of receiving and implementing feedback has been crucial for my professional development and readiness for full-time employment.
